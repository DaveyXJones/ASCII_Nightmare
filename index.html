ASCII Nightmare

You wake alone on an island. Keep yourself fed and watered.

World

How to play

  • Walk: put your left thumb down anywhere on the left side and drag.
  • Act: the big button chops trees, mines rocks, picks berries and cotton, and drinks water. Hold it to keep going.
  • Eat: the small button eats a berry.
  • Craft: the button above makes tools and things to build. Building puts a see-through copy in front of you: tap Build to place it, Done to stop.
  • Walk: WASD or the arrow keys.
  • Act: Space or E chops, mines, picks, drinks and lights your torch. Hold to keep going.
  • Eat: F eats a berry. Menu: Esc
  • Craft: C, then 1 2 3 4 to pick. When building, Space places it and C stops.

Food and water run down as you go. Run out of either and you start to lose health. Nights are dark: make a torch and light it at a campfire.
