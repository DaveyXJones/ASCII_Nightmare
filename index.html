ASCII Nightmare

You wake alone on an island. Keep yourself fed and watered.

World

How to play

  • Walk: put your left thumb down anywhere on the left side and drag.
  • Act: the big button chops trees, mines rocks, picks berries and cotton, drinks water and hits back. Hold it to keep going.
  • Eat: the small button eats something.
  • Craft: the button above makes tools and things to build, and cooks. Building shows a copy in front of you: tap Build to place it, Done to stop.
  • Walk: WASD or the arrow keys.
  • Act: Space or E chops, mines, picks, drinks, lights your torch and hits back. Hold to keep going.
  • Eat: F eats the best food you have. Map: M Menu: Esc
  • Craft: C, then a number key to pick. When building, Space places it and C stops.

Food and water run down as you go. Run out of either and you start to lose health. Nights are dark, and things move in the dark: make a torch and light it at a campfire.
